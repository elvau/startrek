Partner links (advertising)

Split&Fly is a free project. Some links to providers (for example flights via Aviasales/Travelpayouts, activities via Viator) are partner links; each one is marked “Partner*” in the app. If a booking is made through such a link, I receive a small commission from the provider. The price for you stays the same.
